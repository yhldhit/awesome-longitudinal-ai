# A Survey on Diffusion Models for Time Series and Spatio-Temporal Data

## Citation

Yang, Y., Jin, M., Wen, H., Zhang, C., Liang, Y., Ma, L., Wang, Y., Liu, C., Yang, B., Xu, Z., Pan, S., and Wen, Q. A Survey on Diffusion Models for Time Series and Spatio-Temporal Data. ACM Computing Surveys, 2026.

## Link

- Paper: https://dl.acm.org/doi/epdf/10.1145/3783986

## Problem

This survey reviews how diffusion models are used for time series and spatio-temporal data, covering generative modeling, imputation, forecasting, anomaly detection, and other downstream tasks across structured temporal domains.

## Data

The paper is a survey rather than an original benchmark study. It discusses time series and spatio-temporal applications across healthcare, recommendation, climate, energy, audio, traffic, and related domains.

## Method

The survey organizes diffusion-model literature for temporal data by separating time series from spatio-temporal settings and then structuring the field by model category, task type, data modality, and application domain. It also highlights practical challenges and opportunities for future work.

## Key Results

- Provides a broad survey of diffusion models for temporal and spatio-temporal data mining.
- Covers both generative and downstream uses such as forecasting, imputation, anomaly detection, and inference.
- Organizes the literature by task, modality, and domain, which makes the field easier to navigate.
- Includes healthcare among the key application areas relevant to longitudinal AI.

## Relevance to Longitudinal AI

This paper is indirectly but strongly relevant. It provides a broad background on temporal diffusion modeling, which is useful for organizing longitudinal forecasting, imputation, and generative trajectory work in healthcare and multimodal patient data.
