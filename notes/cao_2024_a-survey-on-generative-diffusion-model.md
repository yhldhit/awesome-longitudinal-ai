# A Survey on Generative Diffusion Model

## Citation

Cao, H., Tan, C., Gao, Z., Chen, G., Heng, P.-A., and Li, S. Z. A Survey on Generative Diffusion Model. TKDE, 2024.

## Link

- Paper: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2209.02646.pdf
- Project: https://github.com/chq1155/A-Survey-on-Generative-Diffusion-Model

## Problem

This paper surveys the development of generative diffusion models, summarizing their mathematical foundations, modeling variants, training strategies, and application areas.

## Data

This is a survey paper and does not focus on a single dataset. It reviews work across multiple image generation and generative modeling benchmarks.

## Method

The paper organizes diffusion-model literature around core formulations, sampling procedures, conditioning strategies, architectural variants, and downstream applications.

## Key Results

- Provides a structured overview of diffusion probabilistic models and related formulations.
- Summarizes major improvements in sampling efficiency, controllability, and model quality.
- Highlights broad applications of diffusion models across vision and other generative tasks.

## Relevance to Longitudinal AI

This paper is indirectly relevant. Diffusion models may become useful for longitudinal AI in trajectory generation, missing-data imputation, temporal simulation, and multimodal patient-course modeling.
